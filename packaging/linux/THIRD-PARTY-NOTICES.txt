It runs these programs from your system as separate helper programs (they don't come with it):

exiftool: reads the metadata of photos and videos. https://exiftool.org
FFmpeg: renders video thumbnails and AVIF images. https://ffmpeg.org
libmpv: plays videos. https://mpv.io

Built into good-gallery:

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, the typeface. Copyright the Braille Institute of America.
  License: SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Lucide icons. License: ISC.
Place names of towns, regions and countries, from GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org).
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Shortened to towns of 5,000 people or more.
U²-Net-p, the network that finds a photo's subject. Copyright Xuebin Qin et al.
  License: Apache-2.0. https://github.com/xuebinqin/U-2-Net
Face models aren't part of the app. The user downloads them on request, under their own licenses: https://github.com/Flayms/Good-Gallery/blob/main/docs/face-models.md
Rust crates as listed in the source's Cargo.lock, under their licenses (mostly MIT and Apache-2.0).
